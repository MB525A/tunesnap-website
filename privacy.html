Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026 · Data Controller: MB 525A (Lithuania, EU)

MB 525A built the TuneSnap Android application (including its Android Auto experience) as a commercial, subscription-based application ("Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use TuneSnap, why it is processed, which third-party services are involved, and what rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable privacy laws.

Privacy at a glance: TuneSnap requires no account, contains no third-party advertising, does not access or use the Android Advertising ID, keeps your stations, listening history, and saved songs locally on your device, and keeps anonymous crash reporting and usage analytics off by default unless you explicitly opt in.

1. Local Data Stored on Your Device

TuneSnap does not require you to register or sign in. Your selected countries, saved radio stations, listening history (automatically pruned locally after 30 days or 2,000 entries), saved songs (wishlist), theme preference, and chosen external music app are stored locally on your device. Subscription cache and internal diagnostic counters are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

2. Subscriptions, Trial Eligibility & Billing

TuneSnap provides a 7-day free trial for new devices and offers an auto-renewing annual subscription (which may include an introductory trial offer for eligible Google Play accounts).

  • Google Play Billing: All payments, renewals, and cancellations are processed securely by Google Play. MB 525A never sees, collects, or stores your payment card or billing details; the app only receives a purchase token, purchase timestamp, and subscription status from Google Play to unlock access.
  • Trial Fraud Prevention (Google Cloud Firestore): To enforce one 7-day free trial per device across reinstalls, TuneSnap computes a one-way cryptographic hash (salted SHA-256) of your device's Android ID locally on your device and stores only that hash and the trial start timestamp in Google Cloud Firestore. The raw Android ID is never transmitted or stored, and the hash is never used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

3. Optional Crash Reporting & Usage Analytics (Opt-In)

TuneSnap integrates Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase to help fix crashes and understand which features are used. Collection is disabled by default and only starts if you enable "Share anonymous crash reports and usage stats" in the app's Settings screen.

  • When enabled: Crashlytics may collect crash stack traces, device model, Android version, app version, and timestamps at the time of an error. Firebase Analytics collects anonymous usage events (such as station plays, stream starts/errors, listening duration, opening a song in a music app, saving/removing stations or songs, country selection, Roulette spins, trial checkout starts, feedback submission, Android Auto connection, and screen views) along with non-identifying app properties (selected country or mode, preferred music app, subscription status, and whether Android Auto is used).
  • No Ads or Ad Tracking: TuneSnap contains no ads, removes the AD_ID permission, and permanently disables Google Analytics advertising storage, ad personalization, and ad user-data signals.
  • Revoking Consent: You can turn the toggle off at any time in Settings. Disabling it immediately stops collection, deletes any unsent crash reports on the device, and resets local Firebase Analytics data.

4. In-App Feedback

If you choose to submit feedback via Settings → Send feedback, your message is stored in Google Cloud Firestore together with basic technical context so we can reproduce and fix issues: app version and build type, Android version/SDK level, device manufacturer and model, your currently selected radio country/mode, and device language/locale. Feedback is anonymous unless you voluntarily write your email address inside the message to request a reply. Feedback messages are never sent to Crashlytics.

5. Third-Party Services & Network Requests

To play live radio, display track titles and artwork, update the station catalog, and handle subscriptions, TuneSnap makes standard internet requests to the following services (which necessarily receive your IP address as part of normal internet routing):

  • Google Play Services & Google Play Billing – app distribution and subscription verification.
  • Google Cloud Firestore – trial eligibility hash/timestamp and user-submitted feedback.
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Google Analytics for Firebase – anonymous crash reports and usage statistics (only when you opt in in Settings).
  • TuneSnap Station Catalog (gettunesnap.com) – fetches cryptographically signed station directory updates over HTTPS.
  • Radio Broadcasters & Station Metadata Services – streaming audio, reading ICY/HLS or station "now playing" track metadata, loading station logos (including via Google's favicon service over HTTPS), and resolving backup stream URLs via radio-browser.info when a primary stream fails.
  • Country Flag Images (flagcdn.com) – loads country flag icons displayed in the Explore list and Roulette wheel over HTTPS.
  • External Music Apps & Web Search – when you tap a track to open it in your selected service (YouTube Music, Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music, Deezer, or Google Search), TuneSnap sends the song title and artist to that app or website. Your interaction inside that external service is governed by its own privacy policy.

6. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or Switzerland, MB 525A processes data under the following legal bases (GDPR Article 6):

Processing Activity Legal Basis
Subscription verification via Google Play Billing & delivering live radio streams Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b))
Salted SHA-256 device hash for 7-day free trial abuse prevention Legitimate interest in preventing trial fraud (Art. 6(1)(f))
Anonymous crash reporting (Crashlytics) & usage analytics (Firebase Analytics) Your explicit opt-in consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), withdrawable anytime in Settings
In-app feedback messages and any email address you choose to include Your consent by choosing to submit feedback (Art. 6(1)(a)) / Legitimate interest in responding to your request (Art. 6(1)(f))

7. International Data Transfers

Google / Firebase services may process data on servers outside the EEA (including in the United States). Such transfers rely on Google's participation in the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

8. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Local device data: Saved stations, song history, and wishlist remain on your device until you clear history/app data or uninstall TuneSnap.
  • Trial eligibility record: The salted SHA-256 hash and trial start timestamp are retained in Firestore to preserve trial eligibility across reinstalls and can be deleted upon verified request.
  • Feedback: Retained only as long as needed to investigate and resolve the reported issue or reply to you, after which it is deleted.
  • Crash & analytics data (if opted in): Automatically expired according to Firebase's standard retention schedules (90 days for Crashlytics; up to 14 months for Firebase Analytics event data).

9. Your Privacy Rights

Under GDPR and applicable privacy laws, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data associated with your feedback or device, restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdraw consent for optional analytics/crash reporting at any time in Settings.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority, including Lithuania's State Data Protection Inspectorate (VDAI).

10. Children's Privacy

TuneSnap is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA). If you believe a child has provided personal information (for example, via in-app feedback), please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

11. Security

We protect your data using encrypted HTTPS connections for catalog updates, APIs, icons, and Firebase communications, Ed25519 cryptographic signatures for station catalog manifests, and local RSA signature verification for Google Play purchases. Note that some third-party broadcast radio streams still transmit audio over HTTP where broadcasters do not offer HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in TuneSnap or legal requirements. Updates take effect when posted on this page with a revised Effective Date.
