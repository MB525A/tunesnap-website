FAQ

Quick answers.

How does TuneSnap work inside a car? +
TuneSnap integrates with Android Auto and Apple CarPlay using three top-level tabs (Favourites, Roulette, Country), live song titles before you tap, steering-wheel skip controls, and one-tap song saving.
Is TuneSnap available on both Android and iPhone? +
Yes - TuneSnap supports both Android (with Android Auto) and iPhone (with Apple CarPlay) with the same ad-free experience, Global Radio Roulette, and one-tap music app links.
Which music apps can I open saved songs in? +
Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, Deezer, YouTube, and Google Search. Pick your default once in Settings.
Are there any ads or account sign-ups? +
No. TuneSnap has zero banner ads, zero ad-tracking SDKs, no injected pre-roll audio ads, and requires no account.
How much mobile data do live song titles use? +
Titles refresh every 30 seconds on Wi-Fi, slow to every 2 minutes on mobile data, and pause automatic polling when Low Data Mode or Data Saver is on.
How is my privacy protected? +
Your stations, Loved Songs, and 30-day history stay locally on your device, and optional telemetry is off by default. See our Privacy Policy for full GDPR details.
